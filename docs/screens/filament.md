# Filament

![Filament](img/filament.png)

| Area | Size and content |
|---|---|
| Left rail | 140 px wide: four rail buttons (Manual color, Load, Unload, Cooldown) of 95 px, 12 px gap |

| Right rail | 140 px wide: CFS, Extrude (soft) and Retract |

The Back pill is in the top bar; the screen has no title of its own.
